Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

ChordifyHub ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application and integrated services, including our TikTok video posting feature.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Data: Information you provide during registration, such as your email address and basic profile information.
  • TikTok API Authentication Data: When you connect your TikTok account via OAuth, we receive authorized security access tokens, user open IDs, and public account names provided by TikTok. We never see, receive, or store your TikTok password.
  • User Media Content: Guitar video files and chord visual overlays processed or recorded in-app specifically for publishing to social platforms.
  • Technical & Device Data: Basic device diagnostics, app version, OS type, and performance logs for app improvement.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected data solely to deliver and enhance our features:

  • To authorize and upload your requested guitar chord videos directly to your connected TikTok profile using the TikTok Share/Content Posting API.
  • To save and synchronize your custom chord transposed charts, song favorites, and media project drafts.
  • To manage customer support queries and system performance diagnostics.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. Your information is shared only under strict conditions:

  • TikTok / ByteDance Ltd.: Video assets and user access tokens are transmitted securely to TikTok solely upon your explicit user action to post a video. See TikTok Privacy Policy.
  • Cloud Service Providers: Trusted infrastructure partners providing database hosting and secure media transport protocols under strict non-disclosure obligations.

4. Data Retention & Revocation

OAuth tokens associated with your TikTok integration are stored securely in encrypted formats and kept only while your connection remains active. You can disconnect ChordifyHub from your TikTok account at any time via your TikTok Security and Account settings, or by disconnecting within the ChordifyHub settings tab.

5. Data Deletion Requests

You have the right to request full account and data deletion at any time. To request complete removal of your stored data, send an email to support@chordifyhub.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". We process all requests within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

ChordifyHub does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. If we learn that we have inadvertently collected such data, we will delete it promptly.
